## Proposed query-level decisions and statistical gate

One query is an incoming image evaluated against the full candidate catalog. A false skip is a `skip` action on a query whose correct action is `importFile`; a copy hit is a correct `skip` or a correct high-resolution `importFile` after finding the related image, as appropriate to the prespecified action. Candidate recall checks whether retrieval includes every exact-scan candidate needed for the correct action, separately from the final policy decision. Each candidate representation (Photos original, Photos current edit, or local state) has its own actual width and height; the nearest score alone cannot decide resolution coverage.

Positive strata mean copies of the **same visible image**: `renamed_metadata` changes name or metadata only; `compression_downscale` uses ordinary whole-frame lossy compression or scaling; `format_change` changes encoding while preserving the whole image; `edited_representations` copies the **same already-edited representation** (for example its export versus the current Photos edit), never an edited image versus the original; `hard_copy` combines stronger whole-frame recompression, resizing or metadata changes without a composition edit. Crop, tone, bokeh and different burst frames remain labeled negatives when the reviewed truth says import. A 90° rotation is unresolved and cannot be silently promoted to a positive or used to restore retired frozen cases.

The independent sampling unit is `split_group`: all variants and related families from one capture/session count as **one** trial for a given hypothesis, regardless of pair count. Before any scoring, record the family, original-file hash, split group, positive stratum, expected candidates and action; assign calibration and sealed groups by seed `20261003`. Use one prespecified query per split group and hypothesis for the confidence calculation. Extra variants are diagnostic cases, not extra independent trials. A group may contribute to two distinct hypotheses under the allocated family-wise alpha, but never inflate a single hypothesis count. Calibration may tune threshold and retrieval; sealed results may only accept or reject that frozen choice. Any threshold change after sealed inspection needs a newly independent sealed set.

The protocol uses simultaneous 95% one-sided confidence. The predeclared alpha allocation is 0.025 for false skips, 0.005 for candidate recall, and 0.004 for each of five copy-recall strata; the allocations sum to 0.05. For zero observed failures in `n` independent groups, the one-sided upper error bound is `1 - alpha^(1/n)`. To put that bound at or below risk `r`, require `n >= ceil(log(alpha) / log(1-r))`. These are **minimums for the most favorable zero-failure result**; observing errors calls for the exact binomial bound and may require more samples. A zero-error count never proves the underlying error rate is zero.

| Hypothesis | Proposed target | Alpha | Zero-error minimum independent groups |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Wrong `skip` on an import case | upper bound ≤ 0.01% | 0.025 | 36,887 |
| Retrieval of action-relevant candidates | recall ≥ 99% | 0.005 | 528 |
| Renamed / metadata-only copies | recall ≥ 95% | 0.004 | 108 |
| Compression / downscale copies | recall ≥ 95% | 0.004 | 108 |
| Format-change copies | recall ≥ 95% | 0.004 | 108 |

If the 528 retrieval groups are drawn from the five positive strata, this still needs at least **540 distinct positive split groups and 36,887 negative split groups**, with audited labels, before a favorable sealed result can qualify. Each stratum must cover transformation strength, aspect ratio, size, color profile and supported format; the negative collection must include different burst frames, small screenshot text changes, similar compositions, repeated textures and partial visual resemblance. The 100,000-asset search setting must also cover zero-edited, half-edited and all-edited catalogs, up to about 200,000 representations. Neither the required independent groups nor the licensed distractor inventory currently exists in this task's evidence. If acquisition or licensing cannot support these targets, report **unable to qualify**; do not fill the gap with public regression pairs or relax the gate after looking at results.

## Proposed conversion, performance and resource gate

For the fixed model/conversion/preprocessing identity, compare 1,024-dimensional L2-normalized Float32 outputs against a pinned reference: maximum absolute component error ≤ 0.02 and cosine similarity ≥ 0.999 on an independently chosen conversion set. Record model weight precision separately from vector storage precision. The initial preprocessing candidate is full-frame 320×320; neither preprocessing nor a similarity threshold is approved by this document.

On M2 Pro with 100,000 and 200,000 representations, propose hot end-to-end local-query P95 ≤ 250 ms, local incremental asset processing P95 ≤ 2,000 ms, and process peak RSS ≤ 4 GiB. The hot query includes local read, preprocessing, inference, retrieval and policy. Incremental timing includes changed-representation generation and catalog update, excluding unbounded network/iCloud transfer; report those separately. Also report cold start, P50/P95/P99, storage, CPU/GPU, and component times even where this proposal has no numeric gate. Historical 320-path SSCD Large measurements (4.53 ms inference median, 49.60 ms local JPEG-read-plus-inference median, 88.5 MB FP16 model package) only motivate a deliberately wider initial budget; they do not establish these P95s. Raw Float32 vectors alone use about 819.2 MB for 200,000 representations; 4 GiB allows bounded decoding, model, index and database overhead, subject to measurement.

The predeclared engineering settings are one inference at a time, batch size one, two tasks in flight, one decode, one Photos request, 128 MiB decoded bytes, 128-item catalog batches and 200,000 candidate representations. The 30-second request timeout, 5/15/30/60-second retry delays and one-second progress interval keep individual work bounded and follow the existing short-retry convention; they are hypotheses to test, not measured safe values. A logical timeout does not free a physical execution slot until the underlying operation finishes. The conversion tolerances (maximum absolute component error 0.02 and minimum cosine 0.999) are also predeclared comparison settings, not user-reviewed acceptance metrics. All are fixed by the protocol hash before measurement and are not deployed defaults. M2 Pro results do not establish macOS 13 or Intel acceptance.

## Dataset manifest and audit

`python3 scripts/advanced-dedup/research.py check-protocol --protocol docs/research/advanced-dedup/protocol.json --dataset /absolute/path/to/dataset.json` checks the declared numbers and files without model inference. The dataset JSON has an absolute `root` and nonempty `entries`; paths below are relative to that root. Every entry records `query_id`, `family_id`, `split_group`, `split` (`calibration`, `sealed`, `distractor`, `public_regression`), `stratum`, `origin_sha256`, `path`, file `sha256`, `license_record`, `expected_candidates`, and `expected_action` (`importFile` or `skip`). The original hash identifies the common original **by audited provenance**; the tool detects repeated origin hashes under different labels, but cannot prove a fabricated origin identity or an unavailable original. Each license record has nonempty `license`, `source`, and `permission` fields. Each expected candidate separately records `id`, `representation`, `path`, `sha256`, `license_record`, `width`, and `height`; dimensions must be positive and refer to that actual representation. Recorded dimensions and labels still need human review; the byte-hash validator does not decode images or infer truth.

The check rejects missing files, SHA256 mismatch, known public-regression bytes or lineage outside `public_regression`, a family/original hash/split group crossing split identities, and `false_skip` entries whose expected action is `skip`. If the dataset supplies its own `content_sha256`, the check verifies its canonical JSON content and rejects use of the public corpus hash for a sealed manifest. It reports sealed stratum shortfalls; `approved=true` cannot make insufficient data qualify. Its output contains only counts and protocol hash, not sealed image content or scores. The `fixture_manifest_sha256` field binds the frozen manifest **content hash** recorded in the approved spec, rather than the JSON file's raw-byte hash. Keep sealed labels/results inaccessible during calibration. An unapproved draft must be refused by later measurement tools; this R1 command only audits declarations and local bytes.

The approval text binds the exact draft with `approval.protocol_sha256`: SHA256 of the UTF-8 canonical JSON (`sort_keys=True`, compact separators, no ASCII escaping) of the full document with `approved=false` and no `approval` field. Changing any numeric target or resource field invalidates that binding. Keep this approval record and the scored-result checksum with the eventual report. Approval of targets is only one gate; R4 must still establish data, inference, statistical and performance evidence independently.
